# 🛒 Bazar Dor — বাজার দর

**Bazar Dor** is a market price tracking web application that helps users explore the latest prices of essential daily products in Bangladesh. Users can browse products, compare price changes, view market-wise pricing details, and manage their accounts through secure authentication.

The application features a Bengali-language interface, dynamic product pages, interactive price indicators, and a responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

## ✨ Key Features

- **📊 Daily Price Tracking:** Browse essential products such as rice, vegetables, fish, meat, eggs, and cooking oil, with their current prices and percentage changes.
- **📈 Price Change Highlights:** Explore the top six products with rising prices and the top six products with falling prices.
- **🛍️ Product Details:** View product descriptions, categories, units, minimum prices, maximum prices, average prices, and market-wise price information.
- **🔎 Category Browsing & Sorting:** Browse products by category and sort them by default order, price from low to high, or price from high to low.
- **🔐 Secure Authentication:** Register and sign in using email and password or supported Google and GitHub authentication providers through Better Auth.
- **👤 Profile Management:** View profile information and update your name through the profile update feature.
- **🔔 Interactive Notifications:** Receive toast notifications for authentication events, successful actions, validation errors, and protected-route redirects.
- **⏳ Loading & Empty States:** Display skeleton loaders while product data is loading and helpful messages when products or categories are unavailable.
- **📱 Fully Responsive Design:** Enjoy a consistent experience across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
- **🧭 Navigation & Error Handling:** Navigate between pages seamlessly, with a custom 404 page for unknown routes and product slugs.
